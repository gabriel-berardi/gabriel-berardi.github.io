## Recommended FOSS

I am a huge fan and supporter of FOSS (Free and Open-Source Software). Having a global community of highly talented people working together to develop great software tools is simply a wonderful idea.

Here's a list of open source software projects that I have used in the past or that I am still actively using and supporting:

### Operating System

- [Linux Mint](https://linuxmint.com/)
- [Raspberry Pi OS](https://www.raspberrypi.com/software/operating-systems/)

### Productivity

- [Libre Office](https://www.libreoffice.org/)
- [Thunderbird](https://www.thunderbird.net/en-US/)
- [Cryptpad](https://cryptpad.fr/index.html)
- [Peergos](https://peergos.org/)
- [Syncthing](https://syncthing.net/)
- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/)
- [LibreWolf](https://librewolf.net/)
- [OpenCode](https://opencode.ai/)
- [Pika Backup](https://gitlab.gnome.org/World/pika-backup)
- [Borg Backup](https://www.borgbackup.org/support/fund.html)

### Security
- [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/de-de/)
- [Cryptomator](https://cryptomator.org/)
- [Aegis](https://github.com/beemdevelopment/Aegis)

### Privacy
- [Signal](https://signal.org/)
- [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/de/)
- [Aurora Store](https://auroraoss.com/)
- [VSCodium](https://vscodium.com/)
- [Audiobookshelf](https://audiobookshelf.org/)

### Ad-Blocking
- [uBlock Origin](https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock)
- [PiHole](https://pi-hole.net/)
- [New Pipe](https://newpipe.net/)

### Social
- [Lemmy](https://join-lemmy.org/)
- [Mastodon](https://joinmastodon.org/)
- [PeerTube](https://joinpeertube.org/)

### Finance
- [Bitcoin Network](https://bitcoin.org/en/)
- [LiberaPay](https://liberapay.com/)
